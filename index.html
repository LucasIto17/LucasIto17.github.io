About Me

I am an MSc Finance & Accounting student at Imperial College Business School, where I hold an Academic Excellence Scholarship. I graduated from Hult International Business School with a BBA in Finance & Business Analytics, earning Distinction with a 3.92 GPA and the Growth Mindset and Academic Excellence Scholarships.

My approach combines quantitative and fundamental analysis. I lean toward systematic, data-driven models, but I also value macroeconomic and market judgment — the two work best together, not as substitutes for each other.

I work across Python, R, C++, and VBA, and I am currently preparing for the CFA Level I exam.

My MSc Applied Project studies the forward premium puzzle — the failure of uncovered interest rate parity to hold in FX markets — reflecting my interest in empirical, FX-focused macro research.

Outside of finance, I was pre-selected for the 2020 Olympic Games as a competitive swimmer for Brazil, and I bring the same discipline and consistency to my academic and professional work. I also have a personal interest in football analytics, applying similar analytical methods outside of finance.

My goal is to build a career in asset management or macro/quantitative research, in an environment that rewards rigorous thinking.
