About Me

I am an MSc Finance & Accounting student at Imperial College Business School, where I hold an Academic Excellence Scholarship. My undergraduate work — a Bachelor's in Business Administration majoring in Finance and Business Analytics — gave me a rigorous foundation in both quantitative methods and business strategy.

My goal is to develop into a multi-strategy professional, equally capable in macro and quantitative investing. I believe these two approaches are not competing — they are complementary. Combining top-down macro insight with the ability to build and implement mathematical models through programming creates a skill set that is genuinely valuable to any investment firm.

I am driven by curiosity and a commitment to constant improvement. I approach my craft the same way I approach everything I do — with discipline and a desire to be among the best. Outside of finance, I play drums and compete in swimming, disciplines that have reinforced how I think about precision, consistency, and performance.

My ambition is straightforward: to be among the best in my field, within an environment that rewards rigorous thinking and rewards those who keep getting better.

Principle I
Data Drives Decisions
Opinions without evidence are just noise. Every investment view I hold is built from data — and updated when the data changes.
Principle II
Macro and Quant Are One
The best analysis combines top-down market insight with rigorous quantitative methods. I do not choose between the two — I develop both.
Principle III
Always Be Improving
Good enough is not the goal. I hold myself to a high standard, track my progress honestly, and stay committed to getting better at my craft every day.
